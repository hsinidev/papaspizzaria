Papaspizzaria: A Comical Cooking Simulator

Papaspizzaria is a browser-based game where you take on the role of a chef, tasked with managing a pizzeria from scratch. The goal is to create delicious pizzas, manage staff, and build your business to become the ultimate papaspizzaria master.
